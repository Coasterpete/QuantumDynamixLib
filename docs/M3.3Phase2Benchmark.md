# M3.3 Phase 2 benchmark

The closeout measured the existing **Long varied** distance-authored fixture in
.NET 10 Release on Windows 11 x64 (Core i7-14700KF, SDK 10.0.404, runtime
10.0.15). Each of the six 100 m regions uses the same analytic profiles.
For local station `s` in metres and `k = 2π × 6/600`, lateral and vertical
acceleration are `4 sin(ks)` and `2 sin(2ks + 0.3)` m/s², and bank is
`0.15 sin(ks + 0.2)` rad. Longitudinal acceleration is zero, with a
speed of 20 m/s. The initial pose is the identity.

The reconstruction uses a 1 m maximum step, an 8,000,000 work limit,
2e-6 m position and 2e-6 rad frame tolerances, 2e-7 1/m curvature and
2e-7 rad/m unbanked roll-rate tolerances, and mapping tolerances of 1e-8 m
and 1e-8 s. The join tolerances are 1e-10 m/s² for acceleration and
1e-10 rad for bank. The minimum forward speed is 1e-4 m/s, and the fixture
has no permitted curvature steps. The distance trajectory uses 1e-9 s
elapsed-time and 1e-9 m/s velocity absolute tolerances, depth 32 and a
100,000 work limit per build or query.

The quintic fit uses 3e-5 m position and station-drift tolerances, 4e-6 rad
tangent and 4e-6 1/m curvature tolerances, 3e-6 rad banked-frame and bank
roll tolerances, and 5e-4 m/s² per acceleration component. Arc-length
quadrature uses a 2e-9 m absolute tolerance and zero relative tolerance.
Frame transport uses a 1e-9 rad tolerance. The fit requires a minimum
continuous knot order of 2 and minimum parameter speed of 0.5. Limits are
0.3 m between independent probes, 512 controls, 12 refinement passes and
100,000 verification samples.

## Measured effects

- **Step 1** removes the allocation from segment lookup in
  `ScalarTimeline.Evaluate`. Reconstruction allocated **40.3% less**
  and its median elapsed time fell **16.8%**. Quadrature and basis
  counts were identical. The earlier three-run benchmark measured an
  **11.0%** median reduction, from 8.158 s to 7.263 s.
  [Original Step 1 benchmark](https://github.com/Coasterpete/QuantumDynamixLib/pull/1)
- **Step 2** removes the per-node capturing callback from the inner
  squared-speed integration. Reconstruction allocated **72.4% less**
  than Step 1 with the same quadrature and basis counts. The reconstruction
  median was **14.2% lower**.
- **Step 3** reuses eligible motion samples at reconstruction retry starts.
  Reconstruction quadrature evaluations fell **6.0%** and allocation fell
  **5.9%** versus Step 2, while the reconstruction work charge stayed
  the same. Earlier grouped runs measured Step 3 medians of 5.966–6.002 s
  versus 5.731–5.750 s for Step 2. In the interleaved full benchmark,
  reconstruction was faster in five of six pairs, with a median paired
  reduction of 5.5% and a range from 1.3% slower to 14.7% faster. The
  reconstruction-only series alternated which version ran first and also
  found five of six pairs faster. Its median paired reduction was 3.7%,
  with a range from 0.7% slower to 9.6% faster.
- **Step 4** shares a fitted arc-length inversion within each forward
  verification probe. Verification inversions halved. Verification used
  **11.1% fewer quadrature evaluations** and **40.8% fewer spline-basis
  evaluations**. Verification allocated **32.4% less** and its
  median elapsed time fell **29.5%** versus Step 3. The full fit
  allocated **29.1% less** and its median elapsed time fell
  **12.1%**. Reconstruction timings varied between the two builds,
  with identical reconstruction evaluation counts and allocation
  totals within 0.01 MB.

## Numerical verification

All versions converged in six refinement passes with 12,924 verification
samples, 207 control points and 3,025 frame-transport nodes. The original
probe matched historical baseline fingerprints for reconstruction and fit
output, including motion error estimates at 401 stations on both sides and
forward FVD outputs and inversion estimates at every verification probe. The
extended comparison was also bit-identical across the five builds for the
spline knots and weights, fitted arc-length and frame nodes, solver and
reconstruction join diagnostics, and the sampled banking values.

The 600 m fixture supplies banking values at derivative order zero. The
separate cubic-bank regression probe checked first through third derivatives
on a curve with varying parameter speed. Its sided results matched bit for
bit across all five builds and the maximum absolute difference from the
analytic derivatives was 1.53e-16.
